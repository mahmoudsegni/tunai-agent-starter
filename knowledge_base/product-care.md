# Product care

## Nabeul glazed ceramics

Plates, tajine dishes and cups from Nabeul are glazed and safe for food. Wash by hand with mild soap. They are not dishwasher-safe: the hand-painted glaze fades. They can go in a conventional oven up to 180 °C, but never on a direct flame or in a microwave if the piece has gold or metallic paint.

## Sejnane pottery

Sejnane pieces are made by women potters in the north-west and fired at low temperature. They are decorative: do not put water or food in them. Dust with a dry cloth.

## Guellala water jars

Guellala jars from Djerba are unglazed and porous, which keeps water cool by evaporation. Before first use, soak the jar in clean water for 24 hours. A little seepage on the outside is normal.

## Olive wood

Olive wood spoons and boards should be washed by hand and dried straight away. Rub with a drop of olive oil once a month to keep the wood from drying out.

## Mosaic tables

Mosaic tabletops are sealed for indoor use. Wipe with a damp cloth. Keep them out of rain and frost: water in the joints can lift the tiles.
