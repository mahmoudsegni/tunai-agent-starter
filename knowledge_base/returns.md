# Returns and refunds

## Changing your mind

You can return any item within 14 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Write to support@darjasmin.tn with your order number. Return shipping is paid by the customer, and the original shipping fee is not refunded.

Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

## Damaged or broken on arrival

Ceramics sometimes break in transit. If an item arrives damaged, send us photos of the item and the box within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a free replacement, or refund the item in full including its share of shipping, whichever you prefer. You do not need to send the broken piece back.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge until the order is dispatched. Once it has shipped, cancel by refusing the delivery or by returning it under the 14-day rule.

## How refunds are paid

- Paid by card: refunded to the same card through ClicToPay. The bank shows it within 5 to 10 business days.
- Paid cash on delivery: refunded by bank transfer to the RIB you send us, within 5 business days.

We confirm every refund by email with the amount in TND.
