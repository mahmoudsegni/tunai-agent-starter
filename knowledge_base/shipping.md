# Shipping

Dar Jasmin ships from its workshop in Nabeul to all 24 governorates of Tunisia. We do not ship outside Tunisia yet.

## Fees

- Grand Tunis (Tunis, Ariana, Ben Arous, Manouba): 7 TND per order.
- Every other governorate: 9 TND per order.
- Shipping is free on orders of 150 TND or more, after any discount, anywhere in Tunisia.

The fee is per order, not per item. It is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Delivery times

- Grand Tunis: 1 to 3 business days after dispatch, delivered by Aramex.
- Other governorates: 2 to 5 business days after dispatch, delivered by Rapid-Poste.

Orders are dispatched within 2 business days. Made-to-order pieces (marked "made to order" on the product page) take 7 to 10 extra days before dispatch.

## Tracking

When an order is dispatched you receive an SMS and an email with the tracking number. Aramex numbers start with ARX, Rapid-Poste numbers start with RP and end with TN.

## Failed delivery

If the courier cannot reach you, Rapid-Poste keeps the parcel at your local post office for 7 days and Aramex tries again the next business day. After that the parcel comes back to us. We can send it again for a new shipping fee (7 or 9 TND), or refund the order minus the original shipping fee.
