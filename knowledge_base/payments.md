# Payment

All prices are in Tunisian dinar (TND) and include VAT. The total, shipping included, is shown before you pay.

## Card payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard and Tunisian bank cards (CIB) through ClicToPay, the secure payment page of Monétique Tunisie. Dar Jasmin never sees or stores your card number.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 300 TND. Please have the exact amount ready: couriers do not always carry change. Orders above 300 TND must be paid by card.

## Invoices

An invoice is emailed when the order ships. For an invoice in a company's name with its tax registration number (matricule fiscal), add it in the note at checkout.

## Discount codes

One discount code per order. Codes apply to products, not to shipping, and cannot be used after the order is paid.
